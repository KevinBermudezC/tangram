Why is Auth on its own and not inside the Backend?

AI

Two reasons most teams split it eventually:

  • Blast radius. Auth tends to be the highest-value attack target. Isolating it makes it easier to audit, scale, and patch independently.
  • Reuse. Once you have a second backend or a mobile client, they all need to validate identity the same way. A dedicated service avoids duplicating that logic.
Watch out

Direct database connections from the frontend are a security risk. Always go through the backend.
